## Unreleased

- BUG-0027 review fixes. An earlier cancelled task on the same conversation no longer stops the reply card from opening; only the send's own cancelled task ends a handoff. A new chat's first message now also updates the native selection, so returning to that conversation takes its island card back. Leaving the chat with nothing running no longer pops a card for a turn started later from elsewhere: a leave only waits (up to 60 s) while the chat's own message is still on its way. A handoff Home could not take yet is retried up to 3 times; a busy Home stays final. Earlier turns that showed nothing in the chat now count as earlier turns, so Home cannot mistake one for the new reply. Home no longer rewrites its saved state on every update of a long hidden turn. The screen-on-and-unlocked rule is now one helper (`ScreenState`) shared by voice and the island reveal. JS tests, typecheck and lint pass; Kotlin was not compiled and nothing was run on an emulator.

- BUG-0027 retest fixes (`f6a0913f` on `Taku_PR27_Fresh`). A turn that finishes while the screen is off now opens its card after unlock: Home no longer asks SystemUI to draw the reveal, or counts it delivered, unless the screen is on and unlocked, because SystemUI could accept a card on a dark screen that the keyguard then folded unseen. Leaving right after Send (Home, Back or Recents, while the message is still being sent or queued) now hands off reliably: the chat reports its in-flight message from the tap, a leave without a known conversation completes once the conversation is known (including a new chat's thread), and Home waits for the first turn after the ones that existed before the send, so a turn that finished before Home saw it running still opens the card. `assistant/handoff` gains optional `knownTurnIds`. JS tests, typecheck and lint pass; the Kotlin changes and their JVM tests were not compiled, and nothing was rerun on the emulator.

A tap during a send opens the new conversation once the send is delivered. Controller tests cover all three; nothing was run on an emulator.
